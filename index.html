x9m8 Apps

Interactive simulations and tools that accompany articles on x9m8.com. Everything here runs in the browser.

FluidStable Fluids on the GPU: drag to stir, inject dye, view velocity, vorticity or pressure. Reaction–DiffusionGray–Scott on the GPU: coral, mitosis, spots and stripes from two equations. Paint to seed. FlockingUp to 4,000 boids following Reynolds' three rules, with a predator and obstacles. PDF WorkbenchMerge, split, rotate, watermark, annotate, sign, fill forms, OCR and compress PDFs. Runs entirely in the browser; nothing is uploaded. Read the article ErosionFractal terrain carved by hundreds of thousands of simulated raindrops. Gravity LabN-body gravity: drag to launch, load the solar system, a binary star or the three-body figure-eight, and turn G up, down or negative. Double Pendulum ChaosTwo pendulums launched a hair apart, traced live, with a log-scale divergence graph. Cellular Automata LabConway's Life, HighLife, Day & Night, Seeds, Brian's Brain and all 256 elementary rules. Mandelbrot ExplorerDeep-zoom the Mandelbrot set with autopilot, colour palettes and named co-ordinates. Kinetic ShapesEighteen bouncing-ball arenas in spinning polygons: gravity, restitution and rotation on sliders. Hole CityTwo-minute arcade game: steer a hole through a city and swallow everything smaller than you.
